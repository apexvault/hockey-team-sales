# Beta Backlog

Priority is based on completing and validating the lead-to-reorder path. Claude must reconcile this draft with the actual repository and mark each item DONE, PARTIAL, MISSING, BLOCKED, or NOT APPLICABLE with evidence.

## P0 — Control and safety

- Audit repository, branches, documentation, dependencies, environment examples, CI, and deployment configuration.
- Establish reproducible install, lint, typecheck, test, and production-build commands.
- Verify authentication, roles, tenant isolation, and negative permission tests.
- Verify migrations, deterministic seed data, backup assumptions, and rollback path.
- Establish owner gates and reporting files.

## P0 — Core commercial workflow

- Accounts and contacts
- Product catalog and admin-editable pricing
- Invite-only wholesale access and discount tiers
- Quotes and acceptance
- Orders and status history
- Artwork upload, versions, annotations/comments, and approvals
- Designer assignment and customer communications
- Rosters, sizing, names, numbers, and late/add-on orders
- Payment or approved-credit enforcement
- Production handoff, quality check, shipping, and delivery
- Saved designs and reorder

## P1 — Operating leverage

- Searchable admin pipeline and drill-down dashboards
- Team invitations and team-store ordering
- Notifications and overdue-action reminders
- Audit history and activity timeline
- Basic reporting for sales, margin, status aging, and reorders
- Lead capture and routing

## P2 — Expansion

- Automated outreach engine beginning in Review mode
- Additional integrations that reduce manual work
- SMS after chat and email are reliable
- Broader soft-goods categories

## Backlog task template

Each task must include: business outcome, acceptance criteria, dependencies, risk, files or systems affected, test plan, review owner, and definition of done.
